A NOTE ABOUT ME

Curious by default.
Considered by design.

I’m drawn to the moment when something complicated starts to feel simple. I like open questions, generous collaboration, and the little details that make a digital experience feel like it was made for a person—not just a screen.

My practice moves between ideas, identity, and interaction. The common thread is always the same: make it useful, make it clear, and leave a little room for delight.

Good work, good people, good questions.Rakib
